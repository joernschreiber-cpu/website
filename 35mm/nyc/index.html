„A week in August“

I’ve often felt the urge lately to get my old SLR camera down from the attic and take a few pictures again. It's been sitting up there for 20 years. Photography has always interested me, but I never quite found the hang of turning it into a proper hobby. Plus, the flood of digital images that surround us every day has worn me out. Now, I went on a 4-day trip to New York—the perfect time to finally rescue the old Exakta from the attic.
